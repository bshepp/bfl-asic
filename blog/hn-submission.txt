Etiquette / expectations:
- Use the honest title as-is. HN dislikes editorializing and clickbait;
  this title is specific and self-deprecating, which is the good zone.
- Best odds: a US weekday morning (Pacific time). Most submissions get no
  traction -- that's normal and mostly luck (timing + who's online).
- Do NOT ask for upvotes anywhere (fastest way to get flagged/penalized).
- If it gets comments, engage genuinely. Expect at least one "this isn't
  novel" -- the post already concedes that up front, so just agree and
  point to what IS new (the empirical characterization + the dead-core
  detector). Honesty is the whole brand here; keep it.
- If you post "Show HN", HN guidelines want it to be something people can
  try (the repo/toolkit qualifies) and you the author present.
